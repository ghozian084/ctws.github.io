Problem: Evaluate $x^2 - 3x$ when $x = -4$.

Step 1: Replace every $x$ with $(-4)$.

Step 2: Calculate the power: $(-4) \times (-4) = +16$.

📝 Step-by-Step Example

Problem: Simplify $4a - 2b - a + 5b$

Step 1: Identify the '$a$' terms. $(4a) \dots (-a)$
Step 2: Identify the '$b$' terms. $(-2b) \dots (+5b)$
Step 3: Combine: $4a - 1a = 3a$. And $-2b + 5b = +3b$.
Final Answer: $3a + 3b$

📝 Step-by-Step Example

Problem: Expand and simplify $(x - 3)(x + 5)$

Step 1: Multiply First terms: $x \times x = x^2$
Step 2: Multiply Outer and Inner terms: $x \times 5 = 5x$ and $-3 \times x = -3x$
Step 3: Multiply Last terms: $-3 \times 5 = -15$
Step 4: Collect like terms: $x^2 + 5x - 3x - 15 \rightarrow \mathbf{x^2 + 2x - 15}$

🎮 Simulation: Laws of Indices

Why do we add the powers when we multiply? Watch $x^2 \times x^3$.

$x^2 \times x^3$

📝 Step-by-Step Example

Problem: Simplify $\frac{4m^5 \times 3m^2}{6m^4}$

Step 1: Simplify top: $4 \times 3 = 12$ and $m^5 \times m^2 = m^7$. Top = $12m^7$.
Step 2: Set up division: $\frac{12m^7}{6m^4}$
Step 3: Divide numbers, subtract powers. $12 \div 6 = 2$. $m^7 \div m^4 = m^3$. Final: $2m^3$
