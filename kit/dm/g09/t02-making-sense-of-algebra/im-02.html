Expression vs equation

An expression such as \(3b+2\) is only a description — there is nothing to solve.

An equation such as \(3b+2=26\) states that two things are equal. The equals sign is what makes it solvable.

Guided step — the bracket rule

Substitution is like a substitute player coming on: the letter goes off, a number comes on in its place.

Always put the incoming number in brackets. The brackets are not decoration — they hold a negative number together so the minus sign cannot escape.
