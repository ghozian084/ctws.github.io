Guided step — variables and translation

Variable: a single letter x, y, m standing for an unknown number.

Coefficient: the number multiplying the variable. \(4x\) means \(4\times x\). We never write \(\times\) in algebra because it looks like an x.

Translation key: sum / more than \(\rightarrow +\)  |  difference / less than \(\rightarrow -\)  |  product / times \(\rightarrow \times\)  |  quotient / half \(\rightarrow \div\)

From Expression to Equation

M1 · M2
Added activity. The printed worksheet only ever asks for expressions. Objective 1 also lists constructing mathematical equations, so this task closes that gap.
Expression vs equation

An expression such as \(3b+2\) is only a description — there is nothing to solve.

An equation such as \(3b+2=26\) states that two things are equal. The equals sign is what makes it solvable.
