Meeting 1: Intro to Variables (Part 1)

Main Concept Letters as Unknowns

  • Variable: A letter ($x, y, a$) representing an unknown value.
  • Coefficient: The number in front. ($4x$ means $4 \times x$).
  • Constant: A number on its own (like $+ 5$).
  • Expression: A mix of terms without an equals sign ($3x + 5$).

Activity Modeling Unknowns

Hook: "If I buy 3 closed boxes of Bandeng Presto, I don't know how many fish are inside. How do we write this?"

Task: Use the envelope labeled '$y$' and loose paper clips. Model the phrase: "2 envelopes and 4 loose clips" $\rightarrow 2y + 4$.

Meeting 2: Math Modeling with Formulas (Part 1)

Main Concept Formulating Models

  • A Formula links two or more variables with an equals sign.
  • Step 1: Identify constants (things that don't change) and variables (things that depend on quantity).
  • Example: Joglo cost. $C = W + T$ (Total = Wood + Tiles).

Activity The Lawang Sewu Trip

Hook: "How do event organizers calculate total budgets instantly?"

Task: A bus costs Rp 500,000 flat. Entry to Lawang Sewu is Rp 10,000 per student. Create a formula for Total Cost ($C$) for $n$ students.

Meeting 2: Math Modeling with Formulas (Part 2)

Exit Ticket

Meeting 3: Substitution (Part 1)

Main Concept Replacing Letters

  • Substitution means replacing a letter with a specific number.
  • Always use brackets when substituting, especially with negative numbers!
  • Example: If $x = 5$, then $3x \rightarrow 3(5) = 15$.

Activity Testing the Model

Hook: "In football, a sub replaces a player. In algebra, numbers replace letters!"

Task: Use your formula $C = 500000 + 10000n$. Calculate the cost if 10, 25, and 40 students go on the trip.

Meeting 4: Simplifying / Like Terms (Part 1)

Main Concept Collecting Like Terms

  • Like Terms: Same letter, exact same power. ($x$ and $x^2$ are NOT like terms).
  • You can only add or subtract like terms.
  • The $+$ or $-$ sign belongs to the term on its right.

Activity Algebra Tiles Sort

Hook: "Nasi Goreng recipe: 3 eggs ($3e$) + 2 rice ($2r$). Do you have 5 egg-rices ($5er$)? No!"

Task: Use the shapes to group identical items from: $2x^2 + 3x + 4 + x^2 - x - 2$.

Meeting 5: Single Brackets (Part 1)

Main Concept Expanding / Distributive Law

  • Expanding: Multiply the term outside by EVERYTHING inside the bracket.
  • $a(b + c) = ab + ac$
  • Draw the "rainbow arrows" to remember!

Activity The Area Model

Hook: "A farmer has a Sawah $5m \times x$. He buys 3m extra width. New area?"

Task: Draw a rectangle split into two. Area 1 = $5x$. Area 2 = $15$. Total = $5x + 15$. This is expansion!

Meeting 6: Double Brackets (Part 1)

Main Concept Expanding Binomials

  • FOIL Method: First, Outer, Inner, Last.
  • Box Method: Draw a 2x2 grid to find all 4 areas, then collect like terms.
  • Watch out: $(x-5)^2$ means $(x-5)(x-5)$.

Activity 2D Sawah Expansion

Hook: "What if BOTH sides of the Sawah are unknown?" $(x + 3)(x + 2)$

Task: Draw the 2x2 grid. Find the 4 areas: $x^2, 3x, 2x, 6$. Combine to get $x^2 + 5x + 6$.

Meeting 7: Intro to Indices (Part 1)

Main Concept Zero, Negative & Fractional

  • Zero Index: $x^0 = 1$
  • Negative Index: $x^{-n} = \frac{1}{x^n}$ (It creates fractions, not negative numbers!)
  • Fractional Index: $x^{1/2} = \sqrt{x}$

Activity The Division Pattern

Hook: "Java's population uses $10^2=100$. But what is $10^0$ or $10^{-1}$?"

Task: Follow the pattern: $2^3=8$, $2^2=4$, $2^1=2$. If we keep dividing by 2, what must $2^0$ and $2^{-1}$ be?

Exit Ticket

Meeting 8: Rules of Indices (Part 1)

Main Concept The 3 Laws

  • Multiply: $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$
  • Divide: $\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$
  • Power: $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$
  • Watch out: $(3x^2)^3 = 27x^6$ (Cube the big number too!)

Activity Deriving the Laws

Hook: "Gamelan soundwaves overlapping: $x^2 \times x^3$"

Task: Expand $x^2 \times x^3 \rightarrow (x \cdot x) \times (x \cdot x \cdot x)$. Count the x's. Discover the shortcut rule!

Exit Ticket

Simplify: $\frac{4x^5 \times 3x^2}{6x^4}$
