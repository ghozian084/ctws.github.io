Guided Step: Variables and Translation

Variable: A letter ($x, y, m$) representing an unknown number.
Coefficient: The number multiplying the variable ($4x$ means $4 \times x$). We do NOT write the multiplication sign $\times$ in algebra because it looks like an $x$.
Translation Key: "Sum/More than" $\rightarrow +$ | "Difference/Less than" $\rightarrow -$ | "Product/Times" $\rightarrow \times$ | "Quotient/Half" $\rightarrow \div$
